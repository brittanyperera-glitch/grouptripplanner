## (c) Recommended structure

**Four tabs, plus the avatar for Profile, plus one Menu.** The rule behind it: each stage has one thing to do next. That thing is (1) the first card on the screen you land on, (2) a number on the tab that holds it, and (3) one tap away.

### Tab bar

| Tab | Before the group opens | Group stage (votes) | Finalize | Locked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Trip** (home) | "Next up" card if anything is owed. Then the switch with **Just me** selected. Below it: "Before you go" headline card, map, days | "Next up: Vote on 4" card. Switch shows **Our trip** first, provisional, then Just me | "Next up: Save your final list" | Awards takeover on first visit, then the final plan. "Before you go" card stays near the top. "If we had 14 days" folded at the bottom |
| **Group** (badge = my open votes) | Who has submitted (today's Waiting grid). A "Suggest a place" row | **The votes themselves, inline**, region vote first, late suggestions marked New. Below: Friendship stats block, Suggest a place row | Finalize your list, then the same blocks | Awards block first, then Friendship stats |
| **Picks** (badge = new places to rate) | Switch: **Mine** (editable) and **Everyone** (opens once all five submit) | Banner "Ratings are locked" with Vote now, and my picks below it, read-only. Everyone's picks one tap away | Same | Mine and Everyone, read-only |
| **Guide** | Phrases, Food, Fruit, Desserts, Beaches | Same | Same | Adds a **Before you go** section first: everything to book, and every Good to know tip, grouped by day |

**Header:** avatar on the left opens Profile (as it did before the tab existed, `App.tsx:199-205`). Menu on the right.

### Menu

| Item | When it shows |
|---|---|
| Open items (with count) | Only if Brittany keeps the inbox idea. Lists the same things the badges count, each linking to its screen |
| Group stats | Once all five submit |
| Awards | Once locked |
| My profile | Always (a second door to Profile is fine inside a menu) |
| Change days or pace | Until locked |
| Not you? / Start over / Finish and reset (guest) | As today |

### Badge and dot rules

| Signal | Rule |
|---|---|
| Group tab number | Votes I still owe, including the region vote and late-suggestion votes, plus 1 if my final list is not saved |
| Picks tab number | Places approved after I submitted that I have not rated yet (only before lock) |
| Group tab dot (no number) | Awards I have not opened yet |
| Brittany only | Pending suggestions add to the Group number, and appear in Next up as "2 suggestions to approve" |
| Menu dot | Only if Brittany wants it. It mirrors the same total, so it is never a different number from the tabs |
| Next up card on Trip | Shows the single highest item: votes, then final list, then approvals, then new to rate, then unseen awards. Hidden when nothing is owed |
| Buzz | One short vibration when the app opens and I owe a vote, at most once a day. The pulse plays once on arrival, not every 4 seconds |
| Landing tab | Opens on Trip, and Trip's Next up card carries the action. (Alternative: open straight on Group while I owe votes. See Disagreement 2) |

## (d) Where I disagree with her requests

| # | Her request | My recommendation | Why | She gains | She loses |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | An **Open items tab**, and a **red dot on the menu** | A Next up card on the home screen, plus numbers on the tab that holds each action. Menu dot optional | A dot on the menu teaches people to open a settings menu to find their main job. That is two taps and a guess. A number on the Group tab is one tap and says how many. Phone apps put the signal where the action is | Fewer taps. One source of truth for "what do I owe". No extra tab | A single list of everything owed. The optional Menu > Open items keeps this if she wants it |
| 2 | **Three equal blocks** on Group | Votes inline while I owe any. Blocks for the rest | A hub of three blocks adds a tap in front of the one thing that matters this week. Equal blocks also give stats the same weight as an owed vote (today stats sits on top, P3) | The vote is visible on arrival. Fewer back-and-forth taps | The tidy three-block look while votes are open. It returns once votes are done |
| 3 | **Everyone's picks** as a Group block | Picks tab, as a Mine / Everyone switch | Both are the same shape (places by tier). Putting them together keeps Picks useful after lock, when today it is a signpost (P4) | Picks earns its tab slot at every stage. Group gets shorter | Group no longer holds everything social in one place |
| 4 | My trip first, **Group trip second** | Our trip first once it exists | After the group opens, my personal plan is the one that will not happen. The group plan is what everyone should look at | The plan you see is the plan you will travel | One extra tap to see "my ideal trip" |
| 5 | Knockouts should **vibrate** | Keep it, but once a day on app open, and do not rely on it | We have not checked whether Safari on iPhone runs web vibration (worth a 1-minute test on a real iPhone). The 4-second pulse is noise if someone sits on the hub | A nudge that reaches people who never open the hub | Nothing real |
| 6 | **Profile as a tab** (her change today) | Profile on the avatar, out of the tab bar | Profile already has two other doors. It is a fun page you open now and then, not a daily task. Freeing the slot takes the bar from six to four | Bigger, easier tab targets. The Back button makes sense again | One-tap Profile from the bar (still one tap through the avatar) |
| 7 | (Implied) **Suggest stays a tab** | A "Suggest a place" row in Group and at the end of Picks. Approvals go to Next up | People suggest rarely. Brittany's approvals are a real to-do that today has no signal | A slot freed. Brittany sees approvals without looking | Suggest is slightly less prominent. Late ideas may come in less often, which may be fine |

Where I agree with her as asked: the bold locked banner with a button, full-screen vote cards with the reason for the choice, late suggestions as one extra fork with no re-vote, awards as a takeover on first view, top 2 stats on the Profile card.
