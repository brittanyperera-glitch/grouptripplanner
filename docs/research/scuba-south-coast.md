# Scuba diving, Sri Lanka south coast (December 2026) - research for catalogue entry T15b

Researched 2026-10-16 via web search and page fetches. `[unverified]` marks anything I could not source. Prices are published list prices, not quotes.

## Summary

1. December is in season on the south-west/south coast (PADI: Oct-Apr peak; other sources Nov-Apr or Dec-May). Hikkaduwa, Unawatuna and Weligama are all dive bases.
2. A beginner "Discover Scuba" try dive needs no certification, minimum age 10, one shallow dive (max 12 m) with an instructor, about 3 hours all in.
3. Published try-dive prices: roughly USD 60-105 pp (EUR 70 at one Unawatuna centre, USD 90 at another, USD 103 on a Hikkaduwa listing). Sources disagree; no group discount or December price was found.
4. Do not fly for at least 12 hours after a single dive and 18 hours after multi-day diving; PADI's ideal is 24. Weligama to the airport is 2 h 20 in the app's own legs, so schedule the dive well before departure day.
5. I could not source: Google rating/review count, December-specific visibility, Dondra/Tangalle dive sites, or any booking lead time.

## 1. Where and what in December

- Season: PADI blog says the Northeast Monsoon (Oct-Apr) is "the peak diving season along the western and southern coasts", with Hikkaduwa, Unawatuna, Weligama and Matara listed as open. https://blog.padi.com/why-sri-lanka-is-good-for-diving-all-year-round/ (the fetched summary of this page did not give a date for the post body itself; treat as undated).
- Sources disagree on the window: Nov-Apr (https://www.thesrilankatravelblog.com/scuba-diving-in-sri-lanka/, 2017); Dec-May for Hikkaduwa and Dec-Mar as best visibility/calm water (TripBase, search snippet only: https://www.tripbase.com/destinations/hikkaduwa-sri-lanka/). All agree December is in season.
- Centre season: Divinguru Unawatuna is open 15 Oct to 10 Apr. https://www.padi.com/dive-center/sri-lanka/divinguru-unawatuna/ Divers Land says high season is Nov-Apr (search snippet, not confirmed on its page).
- Poseidon Diving Station (Hikkaduwa) is reported to relocate to the east coast Apr-Oct (third-party listing, https://diveplannerpro.com/dive-centers/lk/lk-poseidon-diving-station-ltd); irrelevant for December but shows centres move with the monsoon.
- Visibility: general figures of 10-30 m and an average of 15 m for Weligama (TripBase search snippet, https://www.tripbase.com/destinations/weligama-sri-lanka/diving/). December-specific visibility: `[unverified]`. Sea state in December: "calm water" per TripBase only; no independent source.
- Sites by base (all https://www.thesrilankatravelblog.com/scuba-diving-in-sri-lanka/, 2017, a travel blog, treat as indicative):
  - Hikkaduwa: about 20 sites incl. Deep Rock, Kiralagala and the Conch wreck. Hikkaduwa Marine National Park is a shallow fringing reef, average depth about 5 m, 60 coral species, 170 reef fish (https://ceylonexpeditions.com/destinations/national-parks/hikkaduwa-marine-national-park-sri-lanka, search snippet).
  - Unawatuna/Galle: rocky formations, black coral, "many shipwrecks". Divinguru lists 16 sites (reef, rock, wreck, deep, night), 6 to 32 m. https://www.padi.com/dive-center/sri-lanka/divinguru-unawatuna/ Specific Unawatuna/Galle wreck names: `[unverified]`.
  - Weligama/Mirissa: Mirissa Point, Bridge Rock, Yala Rock, Prince Heinrich Patch; The Basses (about 40 km reef) called the best site in Sri Lanka (same blog). Travel time to The Basses: `[unverified]`.
  - Dondra, Tangalle: no dive-site source found. `[unverified]`
- Wrecks vs reefs: both exist (wrecks near Hikkaduwa/Galle, reefs and rock at Weligama). A beginner try dive is shallow (max 12 m), so reef/shallow site, not a wreck: https://travel.padi.com/dive-course/sri-lanka/padi-discover-scuba-diving-for-beginners-12324

## 2. Who can do it

- Try dive ("Discover Scuba"): no certification needed, minimum age 10, depth max 12 m under direct PADI professional supervision, equipment included. https://travel.padi.com/dive-course/sri-lanka/padi-discover-scuba-diving-for-beginners-12324 and https://diveforyou.com/index.php/price-list/ (min age 10).
- Open Water Diver course takes 4 days (https://diveforyou.com/index.php/price-list/), so it does not fit a south-coast leg that is a few nights. A 4-dive "Open Water Referral" (EUR 270) assumes theory/pool done beforehand. https://divers-land.net/prices/
- Medical: PADI's Discover Scuba questionnaire says a "yes" to any item means seeing a doctor first, and a yes does not automatically bar you; asthma, heart conditions, a current cold and being under the influence of alcohol or drugs are named. Form wording varies by version. https://wordpress.pgcruises.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Discover-SCUBA-Beginners-Medical-Questionnaire-FORM-2024-V2.pdf (a cruise operator's copy of the form; the centre will supply its own). Pregnancy: not confirmed in sources. `[unverified]`
- Certified divers in the group can do fun dives: Divers Land EUR 35 per boat dive; Dive For You USD 45. Bring cert cards.

## 6. Safety

- Flying after diving: DAN/UHMS workshop consensus is a minimum 12-hour surface interval after a single no-decompression dive and 18 hours after multi-day repetitive diving; DAN had earlier proposed a flat 24 hours but that was not adopted. https://dan.org/health-medicine/health-resource/health-safety-guidelines/guidelines-for-flying-after-diving/ PADI is reported as 12 h single, 18 h multiple, 24 h ideal (secondary summary; PADI page not opened).
- App check: Weligama to the airport (BIA) is 2 h 20 in src/data/legs.json. Do not dive on the final full day; dive on the second-to-last day or earlier. The route's departure day is not known to me.
- Do not dive under the influence of alcohol or drugs (PADI form wording, link in section 2). Do not drink the night before is common advice but `[unverified]` in these sources.
- Local regulations: `[unverified]` (no Sri Lankan dive law source found). Depth limit for try dives 12 m.
- Marine park: Hikkaduwa is a national marine park; any park fees or rules `[unverified]`.

### 8.3 Diving at each

- Unawatuna/Galle: Divinguru lists 16 sites, 6 to 32 m, most reef sites to about 18 m; wrecks Rangoon (32 m), SS Orestes (24 m), Galle Ship Wreck (13 m). https://travel.padi.com/dive-trip/sri-lanka/fun-diving-in-unawatuna-galle-12409 . Centre open 15 Oct - 10 Apr (same page). A shallow reef/rock site suits a try dive; wrecks need certification-level depth.
- Hikkaduwa: shallow fringing reef, average depth about 5 m (marine park; section 1), wrecks by boat to Galle (Dive For You Galle trips, $100 for two dives, minimum 4 divers, https://diveforyou.com/index.php/price-list/).
- Tangalle: Tangalle Reef, beginner-friendly, to 18 m, turtles, Nov-Apr visibility 8-18 m (single aggregator, https://www.diveplannerpro.com/spots/lk/sri-lanka/sri-lanka-tangalle-reef ; unverified beyond that).
- Mirissa/Weligama: rocks and The Basses per the 2017 blog (section 1); a dive-centre base was not sourced.
- December conditions: all of these are in season (section 1); site-specific December visibility `[unverified]`.

### 8.4 Recommendation

Use Unawatuna/Galle, entered with `hub: "Weligama"` and `sideTrip: 1.5`. Reasons from the numbers: it is the nearest base with a sourced, PADI-listed dive centre (Mirissa is nearer by 55 min round trip but has no operator I could source); it is 40 min shorter round trip than Hikkaduwa and 58 min shorter than Tangalle; its sideTrip matches the two existing Unawatuna/Galle items (1.54, 1.46), and it sits on the way to Colombo. Divinguru also advertises free hotel pick-up that includes Weligama (PADI page above), which may remove the driving from the group's day if confirmed at booking. Weligama as a hub is approximate for the dive site (about 45 min away), accurate for where the group sleeps. Caveat: day 7 already holds Galle Fort and Jungle Beach (same area); the planner charges each its own sideTrip, so it over-counts the shared trip, which is conservative.

## 9. Levels (T15c)

- Try dive (no card): section 2. Divers Land Unawatuna: Discover Scuba EUR 70, 2-3 h; PADI Bubblemaker EUR 35. https://divers-land.net/prices/
- Certified: Divers Land (Unawatuna) boat dive EUR 35, two dives EUR 60, four EUR 110, night EUR 50, prices include boat, tank, weights, full equipment, guide, refreshments; refresher + one boat dive EUR 65, + two boat dives EUR 90. https://divers-land.net/prices/ . Dive For You (Hikkaduwa) USD 45 single, USD 80 double, USD 385 for ten, Scuba Review (refresher) free when booking a double dive, dive computer Rs 1,000/day. https://diveforyou.com/index.php/price-list/ . Divinguru's own price list was not on its page (only a 20% early-booking discount); resellers show about USD 51-52 for one dive (not the centre's page).
- Duration: Divinguru PADI Adventures fun dive trip is "about 4 hours, incl. 2 dives", open water certification required, minimum age 12, no minimum logged dives, qualification details brought, dive site chosen on the day, dive computer rental GBP 9 not included. https://travel.padi.com/dive-trip/sri-lanka/fun-diving-in-unawatuna-galle-12409 . Dive For You Galle trip: depart Hikkaduwa by boat in the morning, back at lunchtime (double), afternoon (triple).
- Refresher: PADI guidance is that a diver with no dive in six months should take a ReActivate refresher (secondary source: https://www.scuba.com/blog/padi-reactivate-refresher-course/ ; atlasandboots.com says do not leave it beyond a year). No centre page states its own refresher rule, card or logbook check: `[unverified]`; ask the centre. Medical declaration: the PADI form in section 2 applies to try dives; certified-diver medical requirement `[unverified]`.
- What changes by level: try dive max 12 m, shallow reef; certified trips go to 6-32 m sites including wrecks (Divinguru page above).
- Mixed levels on one boat: no centre page states it. Divinguru says reef/rock/wreck dives suit "all skill levels from beginners to experienced divers" and max 5 divers per guide (https://www.padi.com/dive-center/sri-lanka/divinguru-unawatuna/); Sun Diving says dives are tailored to certification level (search snippet, page not opened). Treat as: separate dives by level, possibly same boat; confirm with the centre.
- Flying: DAN minimum 12 h after a single no-decompression dive, 18 h after multi-day repetitive diving; decompression dives need substantially longer. https://dan.org/health-medicine/health-resource/health-safety-guidelines/guidelines-for-flying-after-diving/ (fetched 2026-10-16). Dive on the last full day only if the flight is 18+ h away; seeded plan flies day 9 after Colombo on day 8.
