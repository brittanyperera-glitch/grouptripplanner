## Fruit

Best place to get fruit, in my opinion, is the roadside stalls. They're literally everywhere and, depending on who you talk to, they may or may not be clean? But also, when in Rome. It's delish, but eat at your own risk.

- **Jackfruit**: Try it before you knock it. Ik there's cooked jackfruit here in burgers and stuff but it's unbelievably better when it's ripe.
- **Rambutan (South Asian version of lychee)**: Lush.
- **Mangosteen**: My fave Sri Lankan fruit. Kinda messy to eat but please have one, they're the GOAT.
- **Mango**: >>>> Tesco mangos. You can also get raw mango mixed with chilli and salt at the roadside stalls. V common snack.
- **Guava**: There's white or pink guava, try both. The pink ones are much sweeter than the white ones.
- **King coconut (orange coconut)**: The coconut water is a popular drink and these ones are deffo safe from the roadside (cause the shell, obvs). One hack: when you finish it, ask them to crack it open. There's loads of really soft, sweet coconut flesh inside which you can eat and it is lush. They even make you a little spoon out of coconut shell to eat it with.
- **Durian**: Stinky. Not for me. Might be for y'all.
- **Bananas**: 29 types of banana?! Collect them all.
- **Wood apple (divul)**: Banging juice fruit.
- **Soursop (anoda)**: Top 3 fruits of all time.
- **Rose apple (jambu)**: Mid tbh, like a crunchy water balloon.
- **Pineapple**: Not sad supermarket ones.

## Food to try

- **Kottu roti**: Final supper type food. Nothing else to say really. Chopped good shit.
- **Hoppers (with or without egg)**: Similar to a pancake batter, it's quite crispy and you have it with a chilli sambal. So nice.
- **String hoppers**: Noodle nest ting.
- **Pol roti**: Coconut flatbread with lunu miris (spicy onion chilli sambal). Simple but slaps.
- **Rice and curry**: Yo you gotta eat with your hands tho.
- **Short eats**: Short eats is the name for any snack you see on the way to somewhere. They're sweet or savoury and they're stunning. Loads of veggie options too. The best veggie ones are:
  - *Elawolu roti* (roti parcels of flavoured potato and veggies)
  - *Bithara roti* (same but with egg for the prote)
  - *Elawolu paan* (veggie stuffed homemade bread)
  - *Rolls* (veg and potato mix, wrapped in a thin pancake, dipped in golden breadcrumbs and fried. Unreal.)
  - *Cutlets* (same, minus the pancake, and small balls)
  - *Ulundu wade* (donut shaped, savoury tings)

  Honestly so many of these I can't list them all, but you'll see them.
- **Anything devilled**: It just means fried and tossed in what is basically a sweet and sour sauce, but with more flavour and much nicer.
- **Sri Lankan fried rice**: Slightly different to regular fried rice but not hugely.
- **Seafood**: Crab curry, prawn curry, fish curry all bangers.
- **Jaffna crab curry**: Northern speciality, properly spicy. Wear a bib.
- **Curry, must-try veggie dishes**:
  - Wattaka (pumpkin)
  - Batu moju (fried aubergine)
  - Pol sambal (chilli coconut)
  - Bandaka (okra)
  - Ule thel thala (fried potato)
  - Beetroot curry
  - Bonchi (bean curry)

  Again, loads and loads.

## Desserts

- **Buffalo curd with kitul pani**: Not really a dessert, but they always sell buffalo curd on the side of the road, which I personally love, and it slaps with kitul pani, which is like maple syrup but made from palm trees.
- **Wattalapan**: Basically a crème brûlée but made with coconut milk.
- **Dodol**: I don't love it but a lot of people do. Like a gelatinous fudge?
- **Helapa**: I like this but a lot of people don't, ahah. Sweet dough wrapped in banana leaf (the leaf is for steaming, don't eat it).
- **Bibikkan**: Coconut cake.
- **Kiri toffee**: Fudge but with coconut milk.
- **The palm sugar pancake one**: Don't know what they're called but palm sugar n coconut all wrapped up in a pancake. You'll know if you see it ;)
- **Kokis**: I'm not the biggest fan, kinda just deep fried batter. Vessel for oil.
- **Pani walalu**: Wayyy too sweet imo.

## Beach spots

- **Hikkaduwa beach**: Very nice area, good seafood places, good for snorkelling (can hire the gear on the beach) and sometimes have turtles hatching in local sanctuaries.
- **Arugam Bay beach**: Famous for the waves and surfing. Stunning beach, quite touristy, loads of cool, edgy Sri Lankan surf dudes that will surf with you. Very hot.
- **Trincomalee beaches**: Beware tiny jellyfish.
- **Pigeon Island**: This is THEE spot for snorkelling, nice coral reefs and sometimes have blacktip reef sharks you can swim with. Haven't got bitten so far so I think it's safe. Gotta hire a boat to get there but that's cheaps and often.
