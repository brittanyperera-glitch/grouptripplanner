# Guide: packing list and phrases (DRAFT for Brittany to check)

## Packing list (draft, December, beaches + hill country + temples + safari)

- Light, breathable clothes: it is hot and humid on the coast and in the Cultural Triangle.
- One warm layer: hill country nights (Nuwara Eliya, Ella) get cold.
- Rain jacket or small umbrella: December is wet season in the north and east.
- Temple outfit: something that covers shoulders and knees, plus shoes you can slip off (you go barefoot inside).
- Swimwear, and a quick-dry towel.
- Sunscreen, hat and sunglasses.
- Insect repellent (mosquitoes at dusk).
- Plug adapter: Sri Lanka uses type D and type G sockets, 230 V.
- Power bank for long drives and train days.
- Basic meds: rehydration salts, anti-diarrhoea tablets, antihistamines, plasters.
- Hand sanitiser and tissues (not every toilet has paper).
- Comfy shoes with grip for Pidurangala and Ella Rock.
- Small dry bag for boat trips and rafting.
- Day pack.
- Some cash in rupees (LKR) for roadside stalls and tuk-tuks; cards work in most hotels and bigger restaurants.
- Copies of your passport and travel authorisation, saved on your phone.
