# North / east coast + extras research (searched 2026-10-14)

Evidence quality: WebSearch returned mostly travel blogs and booking pages, few 2025-2026 sources. Anything not directly sourced is tagged [unverified]. Fees are indicative; confirm on arrival.

## Part 1: North and east coast

### Jaffna town (Nallur Kandaswamy Kovil, Fort, library, food)
- What: Tamil Hindu cultural centre. Nallur temple, Dutch/Portuguese fort, rebuilt public library, crab curry, odiyal kool.
- Nallur hours: approx 5am-12pm and 4pm-9pm; inner sanctum only at pooja times; men topless inside, knees covered for all, shoes off, photography restricted (wondersofceylon.com/nallur-kandaswamy-temple, undated). Best 5-6am or ~6pm pooja.
- Jaffna Fort: $5 foreigner (tuktukrental.com/sri-lanka-entrance-fees, page says verified 30 Mar 2026). Hours [unverified].
- Library, museum hours/fees [unverified]. Allow 1-1.5 days for the town.
- December: land-based, not sea-dependent, so the most reliable north option. Rain showers [unverified].
- Ethics: war-memorial sensitivity; dress modestly; ask before photographing people.
- Commons: "Nallur Kandaswamy temple Jaffna", "Jaffna Fort"

### Delft Island (Neduntheevu)
- What: remote island with wild ponies, baobab, Dutch fort, Hanuman footprint, coral-stone walls.
- Logistics: Navy ferry from Kurikadduwan, LKR 100pp, 8 or 9am out, 14:30 back, max 100 passengers, arrive 30 min early; bus 776 from Jaffna 6:30 (LKR 90) or tuk-tuk ~LKR 2000, ~1h to harbour; island tuk-tuk tour LKR 1500-2500, 4-5h (thecommonwanderer.com/blog/guide-to-delft-island-jaffna, undated; jetwinghotels.com Delft page, undated). Full day ~9 hours.
- Foreigner fee / permit: none found [unverified]. Bring water and food; limited food, faint signal.
- December: no source on December sea state. Ferry is a Navy service and a 2023 Daily News piece reports ferry service problems (dailynews.lk/2023/11/03/local/208432). Treat cancellation risk as real in the NE monsoon [unverified for December].
- Ethics: do not feed or ride the ponies; locals have ferry priority.
- Commons: "Delft Island Sri Lanka", "Neduntheevu pony"

### Nagadeepa / Nainativu
- What: island with Nagadeepa Buddhist vihara and Nagapooshani Amman Hindu kovil, a short walk apart.
- Boat from Kurikadduwan ~20-30 min, ~LKR 50-100, no fixed schedule (migrationology.com Nainativu pilgrimage; lakpura.com/pages/nagadeepa-viharaya; undated). No separate foreigner fare found.
- December sea state [unverified]. Dress modestly. Half day.
- Commons: "Nainativu Nagadeepa", "Nagapooshani Amman temple"

### Point Pedro / Casuarina beach
- No usable source found in this pass [unverified]. Not recommended without a source.

### Mannar / Adam's Bridge (Sri Lanka side)
- Talaimannar old lighthouse at start of the shoal chain; Mannar baobab (~19.5m girth) (sundaytimes.lk/130519 article, 2013, dated; amazinglanka.com/wp/mannar-island). Sandy-road access. Long drive [unverified]. Low payoff for a group; skip.
- Commons: "Mannar baobab", "Talaimannar lighthouse"

### Trincomalee (Koneswaram, Fort Frederick)
- Koneswaram: cliff-top Hindu temple; shoulders and knees covered, shoes off (Rs 20 rack), hours conflict (6am-8pm vs 6am-6pm), entry free or ~Rs 50 depending on source; early morning best (wondersofceylon.com/koneswaram-temple; traveltriangle.com/blog/koneswaram-temple). Fort Frederick is an active army base; the road to the temple passes through it. 1.5-2h.
- December: land-based, unaffected by sea state. Showers [unverified].
- Commons: "Koneswaram temple Trincomalee", "Fort Frederick Trincomalee"

### Pigeon Island snorkelling (from Nilaveli)
- What: marine national park, reef sharks, coral. Tickets from Wildlife Dept hut on Nilaveli beach; one listing says $25 adult +18% tax; others conflict ($48 incl boat per tuktukrental; older Rs 2,765) (amazinglanka.com/wp/pigeon-island-national-park, timeout.com/sri-lanka/things-to-do/pigeon-island-national-park). Boat LKR 3000-5000 return. 2-3h.
- December: NE monsoon (Oct-Mar) makes the sea rough on some days; Navy may suspend boats; one guide says operators resume as the sea flattens around March (championtraveler.com/dates/best-time-to-visit-trincomalee-lk). No source says the park is closed. A December trip is a gamble; check with an operator the week before.
- Ethics: no coral touching or fish feeding.
- Commons: "Pigeon Island Nilaveli"

### Nilaveli beach
- December swell likely [unverified]. Not worth a long detour in December.

### Whale and dolphin trips from Trinco
- Trincomalee season is roughly March-August (jetwingeco.com sightings log 2010-2012, dated). December whale season is on the south coast (Mirissa Dec-Apr). Do not offer Trinco whales for December.
- Commons: "blue whale Sri Lanka"

### Mihintale (near Anuradhapura)
- Search returned nothing usable. tuktukrental lists Rs 1000, not labelled foreigner-only (verified Mar 2026). Hours, climb time [unverified].
- Commons: "Mihintale"

### Recommended shortlist (Part 1)
1. Jaffna town day (Nallur at dawn, fort, library, crab curry): reliable, not sea-dependent.
2. Delft Island day: the adventurous pick, but with a ferry-cancel fallback.
3. Nagadeepa / Nainativu half day.
4. Trincomalee town + Koneswaram: land-based, works in December.
5. Pigeon Island: weather-dependent optional.
Cut: Mannar/Adam's Bridge (long drive), Trinco whales (wrong season), Nilaveli beach (December sea), Point Pedro (no source).

## Part 2: Extras

### (a) Temples
- Wewurukannala Vihara (Dickwella): ticket counter closes 6:30pm, site 7pm; fee not found, "small fee" (lakpura.com/nl/pages/wewurukannala-vihara). ~45-60 min [unverified].
- Buduruwagala (Wellawaya): $3 shrine entry (airial.travel; tuktukrental Mar 2026). Hours [unverified]. ~45 min.
- Aukana: Rs 1000 (tuktukrental, spelt Avukana, Mar 2026); Time Out says Rs 3,250 and 7am-5:30pm, Cinnamon Air says 24h; conflicting. Dawn best per Lonely Planet. Modest dress; ticket office at top of first steps (timeout.com/sri-lanka/attractions/aukana). ~45 min.
- Commons: "Wewurukannala Vihara", "Buduruwagala", "Aukana Buddha"

### (b) Turtles
- Rekawa Turtle Watch: night watch, fee Rs 500 vs Rs 1000 (conflict), hours reported 6pm-midnight or 8pm-1am; return next night free if none seen (reddottours.com/sri-lanka-activities/rekawa-turtle-watch; experiencetravelgroup.com blog). December: green turtles nest year-round, peak Jan-Mar; loggerhead peak Nov-Jan (bluelankatours.com). Likelihood decent but no December count [unverified]. Booking method [unverified]; one listing gives +94 77 790 2915, unverified. Rules (no flash, torches, phones) [unverified].
- Hikkaduwa wild-turtle snorkel: turtles lured with hand-fed seaweed, tourists reported manhandling them (wanderlog; airial.travel; Wikivoyage). No welfare study found. Recommend not listing, or list with an ethics warning. Reef is a protected national park.
- Commons: "Rekawa turtle", "green sea turtle Sri Lanka"

### (c) Tea estates, Nuwara Eliya
- Blue Field: ~Rs 500 entry or USD 3-6 tour+tasting, Mon-Sat ~08:00-17:00, reduced Sunday (lakpura.com/pages/bluefield-tea-gardens, snippet only; fetch 403). 90 min-2.5h.
- Damro Labookellie: no fee or hours found; ~40 min factory tour with tea lounge (byfood.com listing). Free or not [unverified].
- Verdict: Blue Field for quieter; Labookellie as the easy roadside stop.
- Commons: "Labookellie tea", "Nuwara Eliya tea plantation"
