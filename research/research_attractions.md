### Mirissa whale-watching ethics
- **Licensing:** whale-watching vessels need an annual licence under the Sea Mammals Regulations 2012.
  About 60 DWC permits had been issued as of 2022 (themorning.lk, 3 Mar 2022).
- **Harassment is common:** a Pearl Protectors report (Nov 2024) found 73% of Mirissa operators engaged
  in illegal swim-with-whales trips. A viral video of boats chasing whales led to a ministerial
  crackdown pledge in Oct 2025 (The Morning).
- **Operator named for responsible practice:** Raja and the Whales. It is the original Mirissa
  blue-whale operator, follows international guidelines and shares data with researchers, per
  thewildlifediaries.com (2025) and others. This is a blogger endorsement; we did not find an
  independent certification.
- **What to ask any operator:**
  - Do they stay at least 100 m away?
  - Do they avoid boat clusters, approaching the whale from the side rather than chasing it?
  - Do they refuse to offer swimming with whales?
  - Do they carry life jackets?
- **Never book** a "swim with whales" trip. It is illegal under the Fauna and Flora Protection
  Ordinance.

## 3. Calendar

**Poya (full-moon public holidays).** Licensed liquor shops close, and enforcement is active. SLTDA
(Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority) hotels above three-star sometimes get an in-house guest
exemption, but not every Poya: in Dec 2023, all outlets closed on Unduvap Poya.

| Poya | Date | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Il | Tue 24 Nov 2026 | CBSL (Central Bank of Sri Lanka) 2026 bank holidays (cbsl.gov.lk/en/node/19362) |
| **Unduvap** | **Wed 23 Dec 2026** | Same. Also the Adam's Peak season opening. |
| **Duruthu** | **Fri 22 Jan 2027** | CBSL 2027 bank holidays (cbsl.gov.lk/en/node/20573) |

Other dates:
- Christmas Day, Fri 25 Dec 2026, is a public and mercantile holiday (CBSL).
- In Dec 2023, liquor shops also closed on 25-26 Dec, with star-class hotels exempt on Christmas Day
  (dailymirror.lk, 23 Dec 2023). A similar order is possible in 2026 (unverified).
- **Practical:** buy drinks before 23 Dec, and expect Kandy and Adam's Peak to be packed on 23 Dec.

**Christmas and New Year's Eve, south coast:**
- **Mirissa** is the main New Year's Eve beach-party strip, with DJs all night (Time Out Sri Lanka NYE
  guide, undated).
- **Hikkaduwa** has fireworks (Hikka Tranz).
- **Weligama** is a quieter alternative (freedomtravel.se, 2019).
- **Colombo** has hotel parties at Galle Face.
- **Hiriketiya:** one search found no New Year's Eve event listing (unverified).
- 2026 event listings had not appeared in our searches yet.

**Peak-season pricing and availability:**
- Arrivals hit a record 277,327 in Jan 2026 and 258,928 in Dec 2025 (search summary of tradingeconomics
  / German report).
- Arrivals then fell 3.3% year on year in Aug 2026, and the 2026 target was cut from 3.0m to 2.7m over
  Middle East disruption. Late Dec should be busy but not sold out island-wide.
- No hotel occupancy data was found (unverified).
- Common practice (unverified): book 20 Dec-2 Jan beach stays and Yala jeeps or lodges now. Christmas
  and New Year often carry minimum stays and gala-dinner supplements.

## 4. Entry rules, late 2026

- **ETA (Electronic Travel Authorization):**
  - Required before departure. Apply at eta.gov.lk (the official portal; its fee page did not render
    when fetched).
  - **Free since 25 May 2026** for 40 countries: UK, USA, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Germany,
    France, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Belgium, Austria, Czech Republic, Denmark, Finland, Norway,
    Sweden, Switzerland, Poland, India, China, Japan, South Korea, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, the
    Gulf states, Israel, Turkey, Russia and others.
  - Valid 30 days with double entry. Extensions are possible through the immigration portal. The
    previous fee was USD 50.
  - Sources: gov.uk entry requirements ("ETAs are free of charge", fetched 14 Oct 2026); adaderana
    nid=122919 and the Abu Dhabi embassy page (May 2026).
  - Nationalities not on the list: unverified (previously USD 50-60).
- **Passport:** at least 6 months' validity from arrival (gov.uk).
- **Health:** a yellow fever certificate is needed only if arriving from a yellow-fever-risk country
  (gov.uk). No COVID rules. Check TravelHealthPro for routine vaccines and rabies (feral dogs are
  common, per gov.uk).
- **Driving:** a self-drive needs a 1949 IDP (International Driving Permit), obtainable only in the UK,
  plus a Sri Lankan recognition permit (gov.uk). Combined with fuel rationing, use a driver.

## 5. Things to avoid

| Item | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage | Elephants bred and displayed rather than rescued. Born Free's 2010 report: chaining during musth, ankus (hooked goad) training, used as photo props. Responsible Travel no longer recommends it. Use the Udawalawe Elephant Transit Home (rehab and release) instead. | responsibletravel.com (undated); en.wikipedia Pinnawala |
| Turtle hatcheries (Kosgoda strip) | Eggs bought from collectors, hatchlings kept in small tanks, tourist handling encouraged. If you go, avoid any that let you hold turtles. Better: wild nesting watch with a ranger at Rekawa (unverified for Dec). | timetravelturtle.com (undated blog); hidmc.com (undated) |
| Elephant rides, "swim with whales", Mirissa boats without licences | Welfare harm; whale swimming is illegal | See section 2 |
| Time-sinks on a 10-day trip | **Anuradhapura** (full day, spread out; Polonnaruwa gives more for less time). **Wilpattu** in Dec (off-peak). **Minneriya** in Dec (gathering usually gone). **Nuwara Eliya town** (unverified; opinion). The **Kandy to Ella train** had no through service in the latest reports (S1). | Season sources above |
| Rip currents | The FCDO says some beaches have strong surf or rip currents at certain times; ask the hotel before swimming. Rips are a genuine hazard near Weligama's headlands; stay central. The south coast is calm-season in Dec but swells still arrive. | gov.uk safety page; whereismybeach (undated) |
| Leeches | Expect them on Knuckles, Horton Plains edges, Ella Rock and Adam's Peak in wet Dec. Leech socks, salt or DEET. | hikingproject.com Knuckles (undated) |
| Trains | Do not hang out of the doorways; there have been fatalities. Pickpocketing reported. | gov.uk safety page |
| Roads | Erratic driving, worse at night. Always stop at checkpoints. | gov.uk |
| Tuk-tuks | Agree the fare first or use a meter or app | gov.uk |
