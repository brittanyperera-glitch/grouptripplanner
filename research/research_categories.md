# Best-of-each-kind research (Sri Lanka, early-mid Dec 2026)

Method and limits: one standard web search per category on 2026-10-14. Most sources are blogs/tour operators with no visible date; no Lonely Planet / Rough Guides / Tripadvisor ranking page was opened except as search results. Rankings are "most-recommended across the results", not an official ranking. Fees are NOT sourced: shown as [unverified]. Durations and December notes are typical-practice estimates unless a source is named. Verify before relying.

Legend: APP = already in trip app; NEW = not in app. "Commons:" = Wikimedia Commons search term.

## 1. Temples and sacred sites
Sources: https://traveltriangle.com/blog/temples-in-sri-lanka/ , https://www.thrillophilia.com/buddhist-temples-in-sri-lanka , https://bambootravel.co.uk/blog/temples-in-sri-lanka , https://tuktukrental.com/best-temples-sri-lanka/ (undated 2025-26 listicles)
1. Temple of the Tooth, Kandy - APP (tooth). Shrine holding Buddha's tooth relic. 1-1.5h. Fee [unverified]. Dec: open, busy. Commons: "Temple of the Tooth Kandy"
2. Dambulla Cave Temple - APP (dambulla). Five painted cave shrines, 150+ Buddhas. 1.5-2h. Commons: "Dambulla cave temple"
3. Anuradhapura sacred city (Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi, Ruwanwelisaya) - APP (anuradhapura). Ancient Buddhist capital. Half to full day. Commons: "Ruwanwelisaya"
4. Gangaramaya, Colombo - NEW. Eclectic temple/museum. 45m-1h. Commons: "Gangaramaya Temple"
5. Kataragama - NEW. Shared Buddhist/Hindu/Muslim/Vedda shrine, far southeast. 1h+ (long detour). Commons: "Kataragama temple"
Also recurring: Nainativu Nagapooshani (APP nainativu), Kelaniya, Koneswaram Trincomalee, Mihintale.

## 2. Hikes and viewpoints
Sources: https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/best-hikes-in-sri-lanka , https://www.thesrilankatravelblog.com/top-7-sri-lanka-trekking-trails/ , https://www.jetwingeco.com/jetwing-blogs/the-7-most-uplifting-hikes-in-sri-lanka/
1. Adam's Peak (Sri Pada) - NEW. Night climb to sunrise, 5.5 km, 3-4h up. Dec: pilgrimage season opens (Dec-May), crowded. Commons: "Adam's Peak Sri Lanka"
2. Horton Plains / World's End - APP (horton). ~9 km loop, 3-4h, go at dawn. Commons: "World's End Horton Plains"
3. Ella Rock - APP (ellarock). 8-10 km, 3-4h, start early. Commons: "Ella Rock"
4. Little Adam's Peak - APP (littleadams). 4.5 km return, 1h. Commons: "Little Adam's Peak"
5. Pidurangala Rock - APP (pidurangala). 1.5 km one way, 1h. Commons: "Pidurangala"
Also: Knuckles (APP knuckles), Sinharaja (NEW, rainforest).

## 3. Safaris and national parks
Sources: https://www.saltinourhair.com/sri-lanka/national-parks-sri-lanka/ , https://www.atlasandboots.com/travel-blog/best-national-parks-in-sri-lanka/ , https://www.travellocal.com/articles/the-15-best-national-parks-for-a-sri-lanka-safari-tour , https://www.hidmc.com/blog-posts/the-best-safari-parks-in-sri-lanka-yala-udawalawe-wilpattu-compared
Sources disagree on #1: Yala (leopards, busiest) vs Udawalawe (elephants, easiest).
1. Yala - APP (yala). Leopard hotspot. 3-4h jeep. Fee [unverified]. Dec: open, crowded. Commons: "Yala National Park leopard"
2. Udawalawe - APP (udawalawe). Near-certain elephants, fewer crowds. 3h. Commons: "Udawalawe elephants"
3. Wilpattu - NEW. Largest, quiet, leopards harder. 3-4h, far northwest. Commons: "Wilpattu National Park"
4. Minneriya / Kaudulla - APP (elephants: Kaudulla/Hurulu). Elephant gathering peaks Aug-Sept; Kaudulla a good Dec alternative. 3h. Commons: "Minneriya elephant gathering"
5. Hurulu Eco Park - APP (elephants). Commons: "Hurulu Eco Park"
(Gal Oya / Kumana not verified.)

## 4. Beaches, south and west coast
Sources: https://www.hidmc.com/blog-posts/2025-guide-to-sri-lankas-best-beaches-for-sun-and-surf , https://www.onthegotours.com/us/Sri-Lanka/Guides/Best-Beaches-in-Sri-Lanka , https://www.clickstay.com/blog/the-5-best-beaches-in-sri-lanka
South/west coast best Nov-Apr, so December is good.
1. Unawatuna - NEW. Reef-sheltered bay, swimming, nightlife. Commons: "Unawatuna beach"
2. Mirissa - NEW. Whale watching, surf, party. Commons: "Mirissa beach"
3. Weligama - NEW. Beginner surf bay. Commons: "Weligama bay"
4. Tangalle (incl. Rekawa) - Rekawa APP (rekawa); Tangalle NEW. Quiet, turtles at Rekawa. Commons: "Tangalle beach"
5. Hikkaduwa / Bentota - NEW. Hikkaduwa snorkel/nightlife; Bentota water sports. Commons: "Hikkaduwa beach"
In app but not in the lists found: hiriketiya, beachday.

## 5. Waterfalls
Sources: https://www.introtravel.com/blog/10-must-visit-waterfalls-sri-lanka/ , https://traveltriangle.com/blog/waterfalls-in-sri-lanka/ , https://www.yamu.lk/highlights/best-waterfalls-in-sri-lanka-including-hidden-gems/
Heights vary by source (Diyaluma 171-220 m).
1. Bambarakanda - NEW. Tallest (~263 m), near Haputale. 1h. Commons: "Bambarakanda Falls"
2. Diyaluma - APP (diyaluma). ~220 m, upper pools. 1-2h. Dec: lower flow. Commons: "Diyaluma Falls"
3. Ravana Falls - NEW. 25 m roadside by Ella. 20m. Commons: "Ravana Falls"
4. St Clair's - NEW. "Little Niagara", near Talawakele. 20-30m. Commons: "St Clair's Falls"
5. Devon - NEW. ~97 m, viewpoint only (private land). 20m. Commons: "Devon Falls"
Also: Bomburu Ella (widest).

## 6. Historic cities and forts
Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_triangle , https://blog.cinnamonhotels.com/07-must-see-places-in-the-cultural-triangle-sri-lanka/ (no ranking found; Galle Fort not covered by this search, so its place here is my judgement)
1. Sigiriya - APP (sigiriya). 5th-C rock citadel. 2-3h. Commons: "Sigiriya"
2. Anuradhapura - APP (anuradhapura). Commons: "Anuradhapura"
3. Polonnaruwa - APP (polonnaruwa). Medieval capital, Gal Vihara. 3-4h. Commons: "Polonnaruwa Gal Vihara"
4. Kandy (sacred city) - NEW as a city (tooth APP). Last royal capital. Commons: "Kandy Sri Lanka"
5. Galle Fort - APP (galle). Dutch fort, UNESCO. 2h. Commons: "Galle Fort"
Also: Delft (APP delft), Mihintale (NEW), Jaffna Fort (NEW).

## 7. Scenic trains and rail spots
Sources: https://www.roughguides.com/sri-lanka/best-train-rides/ , https://www.wanderlustmagazine.com/inspiration/best-rail-journeys-in-sri-lanka/ , https://luxuryescapes.com/inspiration/most-scenic-rail-journeys-sri-lanka/
1. Kandy to Ella - NEW as a route (app has Nanu Oya-Ella). Headline route, 6-8h+. Book reserved class ahead; Dec is peak. Commons: "Kandy to Ella train"
2. Nanu Oya to Ella - APP (train). ~3h, tea terraces. Commons: "Nanu Oya Ella train"
3. Nine Arch Bridge, Demodara - APP (ninearch). 1h; time it to a train. Commons: "Nine Arches Bridge Ella"
4. Colombo to Galle coastal line - NEW. ~2.5-3h, sea views. Commons: "Colombo Galle coastal railway"
5. Ella Odyssey (tourist train Colombo-Badulla) - NEW. Stops at Nine Arches ~10 min. Commons: "Ella Odyssey train"

## 8. Tea estates and factories
Sources: https://www.bluelankatours.com/blog/best-tea-plantations-factories-sri-lanka , https://www.hidmc.com/blog-posts/sri-lankas-tea-factory-tours-from-plantation-to-cup
Thin evidence; sources name Pedro and Labookellie most. Order below is partly my judgement.
1. Pedro Tea Estate, Nuwara Eliya - NEW. ~20 min tour; processing at night. Commons: "Pedro Tea Estate"
2. Labookellie (ex-Mackwoods) - APP (tea). Free tours, busy roadside. Commons: "Labookellie tea"
3. Blue Field, Nuwara Eliya - NEW. One mention only. Commons: "Blue Field tea Nuwara Eliya"
4. Dambatenne, Haputale - NEW. Itinerary mention only. Commons: "Dambatenne tea factory"
5. Halpewatte, Ella - NEW. Factory tours, tea masterclass. Commons: "Halpewatte tea factory"
Also: Handunugoda (white tea, south).
