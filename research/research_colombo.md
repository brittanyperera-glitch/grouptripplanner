## What to put in front of the group (5)
1. Pettah + Red Mosque (morning) - the single most "Sri Lanka" hit, short, free.
2. Tuk-tuk street food tour (evening) - social, adventurous, covers food and a city overview in one booking.
3. Galle Face Green at sunset - free, atmosphere, no booking.
4. Ministry of Crab dinner - the big-ticket meal; needs booking weeks ahead, so decide now.
5. Rooftop drinks or Lotus Tower - pick one for skyline and nightlife.
Optional: Gangaramaya if on the way. Skip Mount Lavinia on this trip if heading south (beaches on the south coast are better; unverified comparison).

## How long Colombo deserves
Recommendation: about 1 night and 1 full day (roughly 24-30 hours), not more.
Reasoning: airport is ~35 min via expressway [your figure]; Galle ~1.5 h on the Southern Expressway [your figure]. So Colombo is easy to start or end in with little transit loss. One full day fits Pettah (morning), a lunch or Bawa/Gangaramaya, Galle Face + Lotus/rooftop (evening), food tour or Ministry of Crab (dinner). A second night adds only repeats (museum, cooking class, Mount Lavinia), which compete with beach time further south.
Suggested use: as an END stop, arrive from Galle by ~noon, do the sights, dinner at Ministry of Crab, fly out next day with a flight buffer (airport ~35 min but allow for traffic) [timing assumption, not sourced]. As a START stop, land, rest, and do the evening items; Pettah the next morning before heading south (Sunday closes Pettah, so avoid arrival/departure day being Sunday if possible).
