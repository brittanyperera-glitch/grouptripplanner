## 2. Train
Assumption (a) is PARTLY FALSE: Kandy-Ella through-running is NOT operating as of 2026-10-14.
- Nanu Oya -> Ella -> Badulla: reopened (sources conflict: 20 June 2026 per newsfirst 6 Sep 2026; 18 July 2026 per byc.lk). Running. Source: https://www.newsfirst.lk/2026/09/06/train-services-between-peradeniya-and-colombo-fort-to-resume-next-month (pub 6 Sep 2026) ; https://byc.lk/colombo-kandy-ella-train-status-2026 (updated 7 Oct 2026) ; https://www.byc.lk/nanu-oya-to-ella-train-2026 (updated 9/23 Sep 2026).
- Colombo-Rambukkana running; upcountry trains only to Rambukkana per newsfirst (Sep 2026). Ministry said Colombo-Peradeniya would resume "first week of October"; byc.lk on 7 Oct says still no confirmed date. Kalu/Black Bridge Peradeniya-Kandy still under restoration; gaps Gampola-Nawalapitiya and Hatton-Nanu Oya (geckoroutes/search summary, unverified directly).
- Full-line reopening: minister's target end-2026 or Jan-Feb 2027; geckoroutes (updated 10 Sep 2026) says early 2027; a July table said late 2026. => Do NOT plan Dec on Kandy-Ella through train. Realistic: drive Kandy -> Nanu Oya/Nuwara Eliya (~2.5-3h; byc.lk), take train Nanu Oya -> Ella.
- Nanu Oya-Ella timetable (byc.lk): dep 9:00 and 14:30, ~2h15-2h30; Ella-Nanu Oya 9:52 and 15:52. Best scenery segment = this high-plateau section (Ambewela-Pattipola-Haputale-Bandarawela-Ella). byc.lk page has internal inconsistencies; verify timetable on official site.
- Fares (byc.lk only, low confidence): 3rd 300-500 LKR; reserved 2nd ~4,000 LKR; 1st-class observation saloon ~5,000 LKR (gives Ella Odyssey 2,000 elsewhere on same page - inconsistent). Another source: 1,560 LKR reserved Kandy-Ella seat (full line estimate). Geckoroutes: unreserved 2nd from 160 LKR.
- Booking: official seatreservation.railway.gov.lk (per dailynews.lk 14 Mar 2024) ; app. Seats release 30 days before travel (12Go FAQ, geckoroutes updated 10 Sep 2026; byc.lk says 9:00 AM, unverified). Online until 2h before departure. Official site has no calendar view. Classes: reserved 1st (AC / observation) and 2nd; unreserved 2nd/3rd on the day only. Geckoroutes' "4 days ahead" claim contradicts and is doubtful.
- If sold out: station on arrival, local agent (500-1000 LKR markup, may add 2-3x), or unreserved on the day (30-60 min queue, may stand). Tour agents sometimes hold seats. For Dec 2026: set a reminder to book exactly 30 days before each date.
- Ella Odyssey tourist train: status uncertain; Jan 2026 newsfirst said it resumed after cyclone suspension (https://english.newsfirst.lk/2026/01/07/ella-odyssey-resumes-service-today-after-suspension-due-to-cyclone-ditwah) - the article predates the later closures; current schedule unverified.

## 3. Private driver / van
- Minivan up to 6 pax from US$86/vehicle/day (serendiblocal.lk, undated, (c)2026); sedan from $59, SUV $77 (same aggregator via search). Other operators: $50-70 car, $70-100 SUV/van (2026 guide, srilankaprivatedriver.com, via search snippet). For 5 + luggage you realistically need a 7-9 seat van (KDH/Hiace) => expect ~US$90-130/day [E: above the sourced minimum; confirm via quotes]. 10 days ~US$860 at the $86 rate (serendib) – likely a floor.
- Typical inclusions: driver, AC vehicle, fuel, tolls/highway, parking, insurance, ~100-150 km/day; extra km ~US$0.50 or LKR 110 (srilankantours.org/srilankacarhirewithdriver.com snippets). Driver meals and accommodation: Serendib says included; others say meals extra $10-20/day. Norm: you pay for driver's room (guesthouses usually have cheap driver rooms) or the quote includes it - get it in writing.
- Group by private van + driver (assumption b): consistent with all operator pages; no counter-evidence found. Train/bus as a complement.
- Ride-hailing: PickMe is the main app (tuk-tuk + car), Uber Colombo-focused; both allowed at the airport since June 2023 (restofworld.org/2024/sri-lanka-pickme). July 2026 AsiaNews: tuk-tuk driver vs app friction, notably intimidation of PickMe drivers in Ella - expect to rely on street tuk-tuks there; ~LKR 90-110/km tuk-tuk (May 2026 guide).

## 4. Airport
- Arrive 3h before international departure (BIA 2019 notice; flightqueue.com generic). Possible Oct 2025 change: check-in opens 4h before (unverified, all-maps.com). Add buffer for Colombo/Katunayake traffic.
- Airport -> Sigiriya: not sourced directly; Negombo->Anuradhapura 3h45 and Anuradhapura->Sigiriya 1h30 suggest the direct road is about 4-4.5h via Kurunegala/Dambulla [E]; no Central Expressway shortcut.
- South coast -> airport: Galle->Colombo 1h23 expressway + Colombo->airport ~35 min (S) => ~2h to 2h30 from Galle free-flow; Mirissa ~+45 min. Recommend leaving 6-7h before a flight, or overnight near Negombo [E, judgment].
- Assumption (c) Colombo only realistic gateway: true for international (Mattala/Hambantota and Jaffna have limited international service - not researched, unverified).
